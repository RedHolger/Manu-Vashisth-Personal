You are invited to take part in a short usability study (about 20–30 min).

- **What you do:** complete 3 small tasks in a prototype dashboard/planner
  while we observe. Your screen actions and one verbatim observation per task
  are recorded, with the time each task took.
- **Your data:** a pseudonymous id (e.g. `P-003`); task success (yes/no);

  deletion of your records. Withdrawing deletes everything attributed to you.

  participants missed the stale label"). No population or causal claims.
